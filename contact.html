Getting here

We are a two-minute walk from the main bustop. Covered bike racks sit at the front door, and free weekend parking is available for all attendees.

Other ways to reach us

  • Email hello@laeurbanyouthsbikeworkshop.org.pg — we reply within two working days.
  • Call (675) 7123 4567 during opening hours.
  • Drop in during any open session — the kettle is always on.
  • Course dates and workshop news are also posted on the noticeboard at Riverton Library.
